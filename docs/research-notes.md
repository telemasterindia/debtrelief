# Research notes

## Access limitations

The build environment's network policy blocked greenlightdebtrelief.com,
YouTube and the Wayback Machine. The official video (92CTw_kb6x8) is embedded
but could not be viewed here.

## Sources used

- **Owner's brief** — positioning (referral/consultation website), services and
  benefits (More Savings, Dedicated Account Managers, Live Online Access, Top
  Rated, Free Consultation, Custom Plans), help-with categories, process steps,
  FAQ topics, contact details, company history ("since 1998"), video.
- **Official logo** — supplied by Greenlight; brand colours measured from it
  (see `public/brand/README.md`).

## Not used

- A lookalike page for `greenlightsdebtrelief.com` (extra "s") hosted on S3,
  with a different address and a "guaranteed in 6 months" claim.
- Third-party listings (ratings, review counts, addresses) — not verified.

## Scope decision

Educational content (debt validation guides, CFPB/FTC/IRS explanations, law,
tax, bankruptcy, alternatives) was removed from the site at the owner's request.
Old URLs redirect to the homepage.
